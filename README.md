# Supermarket Sales Analysis

## 1. Key Findings

1. The United Kingdom contributes the highest sales compared to other countries.
2. The Netherlands and Ireland are among the other important sales-generating countries.
3. November 2011 recorded the highest monthly sales during the analysed period.
4. Products such as Dotcom Postage, Regency Cake Stand and Paper Craft Little Birdie generated high sales revenue.
5. The heat map and bubble chart help identify high-performing products and countries.
6. Product quantity and sales values vary across different products.

## 2. Conclusion

The supermarket sales analysis provides a clear understanding of business performance across different countries, products and time periods. The visualizations help identify sales patterns, popular products and high-performing countries. Overall, the analysis shows that sales are concentrated mainly in the United Kingdom, with opportunities to improve performance in other countries.

## 3. Suggestions

1. Improve marketing strategies in countries with lower sales.
2. Maintain sufficient stock of popular products.
3. Introduce special offers to attract more customers.
4. Monitor monthly sales to identify changes in customer demand.
5. Focus on improving the sales of low-performing products.

## 4. Recommendations

1. Concentrate more on high-performing products to increase revenue.
2. Expand marketing activities in countries with growth opportunities.
3. Use sales data to improve inventory management.
4. Regularly monitor sales performance through Tableau dashboards.
5. Make data-driven business decisions to improve overall supermarket performance.
